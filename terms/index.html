5. Bookings, meet & greets and cancellations

When an owner requests a booking and a carer accepts it, a booking is formed between them on the terms shown in the app — the service, the times, the pets involved and the price.

Meet & greets

Some carers require a meet & greet before accepting ongoing work. This is a short, no-obligation introduction so that everyone — including the dog — can decide whether it is a good fit. Either side can decide not to proceed afterwards.

Cancellations

  • The cancellation window that applies to a booking is shown to you at the time you book, and in the booking itself. Please check it there — it is what applies.
  • Cancel as early as you reasonably can. Late cancellations leave a carer with an unfillable gap, or an owner without care at short notice.
  • If a carer cancels a booking you have arranged, we will help you find a replacement where we can. Where you have turned on backup cover, we may arrange a Truffl backup carer in line with the consent you gave when you enabled it.
  • We may cancel a booking ourselves if we believe it is unsafe, fraudulent, or in breach of these terms.

6. Payments and fees

  • Payments are processed by Stripe. Your card details go directly to Stripe and are never stored on, and never pass through, Truffl's systems.

  • Any Truffl platform fee is disclosed to you at the time of booking. Carers are shown their commission, if any, before accepting.
  • Carers are paid out through their own Stripe account. Carers are responsible for their own tax, including GST where it applies, and for their own ABN and record-keeping.
  • If a payment fails, we may retry it and will contact you. Unpaid completed bookings remain payable.
  • Refunds are handled case by case — contact us and we will work it out with you and the carer.

7. Your responsibilities

If you're an owner

  • Give accurate and complete information about your pet — temperament, health, medication, routine, and anything a carer needs to keep themselves and your pet safe. Tell the carer about any history of aggression or biting. This one matters most.
  • Make sure your pet is up to date on vaccinations and appropriately registered, and that your dog is safe to be handled and walked by someone new.
  • Provide the access your carer needs — keys, gates, leads, harnesses — and make sure your home is safe to enter for the arranged visit.
  • Urgent veterinary care is your responsibility to authorise and to pay for. Make sure we and your carer have a way to reach you, and tell your carer what you want done if they can't.

If you're a carer

  • Only accept bookings you can genuinely do, and turn up when you said you would.
  • Follow the owner's instructions for the pet, and our carer guidelines, which set out the standards we expect on every booking.
  • Treat every home you enter with care, and use access details only for the booking they were given for.
  • Keep location tracking on for the duration of a walk so the owner can follow along, and share the updates the booking calls for.
  • Tell the owner promptly if anything goes wrong, and tell us if it's serious.
  • Keep your vetting information current, and tell us if anything changes that affects your eligibility.

Everyone

Don't use Truffl to do anything unlawful, to harass or discriminate against anyone, to misrepresent yourself, or to interfere with how the platform works.
